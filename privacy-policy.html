隐私政策

最近更新：2026 年 7 月 8 日

本隐私政策说明「格物实验」（以下简称“本应用”，应用包名 com.zhangfeiyang.gewu）如何处理用户与设备数据。本应用由独立开发者 zhangfeiyang 开发与维护。使用本应用即表示您接受本政策所述的做法。

1. 我们收集的数据

本应用是一款完全离线的互动科学仿真实验室。我们不收集、不存储、不传输任何可识别您个人身份的信息。

具体而言，本应用不会：

  • 请求或收集您的姓名、电子邮件、电话号码等个人信息；
  • 收集您的精确或大致地理位置；
  • 读取您的通讯录、相册、短信或通话记录；
  • 接入任何广告 SDK、分析统计 SDK（如 Firebase Analytics、Google Analytics）、崩溃上报 SDK 或第三方追踪服务；
  • 将您的使用行为数据发送至任何服务器。

2. 本地存储的数据

为提供正常功能，本应用会在您的设备本地保存以下偏好设置（使用系统提供的本地存储，仅存储在本设备，不会上传）：

  • 界面语言选择（中文 / English）；
  • 已收藏的实验、最近使用的实验、探索进度；
  • 触感反馈与深色模式的开关状态。

这些数据始终保留在您的设备上，您随时可在应用的「关于与设置」页面清除，或通过卸载本应用将其全部删除。卸载后这些数据将无法恢复。

3. 设备权限

本应用仅申请以下一项权限，且不涉及任何敏感数据访问：

  • 振动（VIBRATE）：在碰撞、按钮操作等交互时提供轻微的触感反馈。您可在设置中关闭触感反馈。本权限不读取任何数据。

本应用不申请相机、麦克风、位置、存储、通讯录、电话等敏感权限。

4. 数据共享与披露

由于我们不收集任何个人数据，因此我们不会与任何第三方共享、出售或出租您的个人信息。本应用不存在任何第三方数据接收方。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用面向科学学习与教学场景，适合所有年龄段用户，包括儿童。由于本应用不收集任何个人信息，因此对 13 岁以下儿童的使用同样符合 COPPA 等儿童隐私保护要求。

6. 外部链接

应用内的「关于」页面可能包含指向 PhET 交互式仿真项目（phet.colorado.edu）的参考链接，仅用于说明教学理念的来源。点击该链接将通过您设备上的浏览器打开外部网站，届时适用目标网站自身的隐私政策，本应用不再对外部网站的数据处理行为负责。

7. 数据安全

由于本应用不在设备之外处理或传输任何用户数据，因此不存在数据在传输或服务器存储过程中的泄露风险。本地存储的数据受您设备自身的安全机制保护。

8. 政策变更

我们可能不时更新本隐私政策。更新后的政策将在本页面发布，并以页面顶部「最近更新」日期为准。建议您定期查阅本页面以了解任何变更。

9. 联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，可通过 GitHub 与开发者联系：github.com/zhangfeiyang。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how "Gewu Lab" (the "App", package name com.zhangfeiyang.gewu) handles user and device data. The App is developed and maintained by the independent developer zhangfeiyang. By using the App, you accept the practices described in this policy.

1. Data We Collect

This App is a fully offline interactive science simulation laboratory. We do not collect, store, or transmit any information that could personally identify you.

Specifically, the App does NOT:

  • Request or collect your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information;
  • Collect your precise or approximate location;
  • Access your contacts, photo gallery, messages, or call logs;
  • Integrate any advertising SDK, analytics SDK (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics), crash-reporting SDK, or third-party tracking service;
  • Send your usage behavior data to any server.

2. Data Stored Locally

To provide its normal functionality, the App stores the following preferences locally on your device (using the local storage provided by the system, kept only on this device and never uploaded):

  • Interface language selection (中文 / English);
  • Favorited simulations, recently used simulations, and exploration progress;
  • Haptic feedback and dark mode toggle states.

This data always remains on your device. You may clear it at any time from the "About & Settings" page within the App, or remove it entirely by uninstalling the App. After uninstallation, this data cannot be recovered.

3. Device Permissions

The App requests only the following permission, which does not involve any sensitive data access:

  • VIBRATE: provides light haptic feedback during interactions such as collisions and button presses. You can turn off haptic feedback in Settings. This permission does not read any data.

The App does not request camera, microphone, location, storage, contacts, phone, or other sensitive permissions.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

Because we do not collect any personal data, we do not share, sell, or rent your personal information with any third party. The App has no third-party data recipients.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for science learning and teaching scenarios and is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Since the App collects no personal information, its use by children under 13 also complies with children's privacy requirements such as COPPA.

6. External Links

The "About" page within the App may contain a reference link to the PhET Interactive Simulations project (phet.colorado.edu), included only to acknowledge the source of the teaching philosophy. Clicking this link opens the external site in your device's browser, at which point the target website's own privacy policy applies; the App is not responsible for the data practices of external websites.

7. Data Security

Because the App does not process or transmit any user data outside the device, there is no risk of data leakage during transmission or server storage. Locally stored data is protected by your device's own security mechanisms.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated policies will be posted on this page, with the "Last updated" date at the top reflecting the change. We recommend you review this page periodically for any updates.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer via GitHub: github.com/zhangfeiyang.
